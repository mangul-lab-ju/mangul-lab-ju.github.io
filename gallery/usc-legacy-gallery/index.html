Historical photos from the USC era of Mangul Lab

Historical photos from the USC era of Mangul Lab.

During this period, the Mangul Lab was actively part of:

  • Titus Department of Clinical Pharmacy at the USC Alfred E. Mann School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences
  • Department of Quantitative and Computational Biology at the USC Dornsife College of Letters, Arts and Sciences

The gallery features photos from lab group meetings, social events, poster presentations, and international computational biology conferences.
