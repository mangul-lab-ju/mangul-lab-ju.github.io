Biography

Junghyun Jung joins Mangul Lab in 2020 as a new postdoctoral scholar and is co-advised by Dr. Serghei Mangul and Dr. Nicholas Mancuso. He received his Ph.D.in 2020 from Dongguk University, Seoul, Korea. His research interests include integrative analysis using multi-omic data and method development for functional genomic data.
