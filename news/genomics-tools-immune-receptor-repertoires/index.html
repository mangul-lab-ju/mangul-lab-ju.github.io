Developing robust and scalable genomics tools and databases to analyze immune receptor repertoires

The Mangul Lab is opening 3 PhD positions for the M2 project focusing on robust bioinformatics tools and the Immunome Aggregation Database (TIAD).

The Mangul Lab at the Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology is launching a new ambitious project (M2) focused on the development of robust and scalable bioinformatics tools and databases to characterize the adaptive immune receptor repertoire (AIRR) at a population scale.

Current immunogenomics databases primarily represent European ancestries, creating a critical gap in understanding immune system complexity across diverse populations. This research addresses these disparities by developing methods to identify population-specific immune gene variation from large-scale genomic data.

Project Description

The PhD students will work on developing methods to infer novel, population-specific V(D)J gene alleles from large-scale next-generation sequencing (NGS) data, including RNA-Seq and Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS).

A key outcome of this research will be the creation of the Immunome Aggregation Database (TIAD), which is set to become the largest collection of T and B cell receptor sequences across ethnically diverse populations.

Candidate Profile

We are looking for highly motivated PhD candidates with:

  • A strong academic background in computational biology, computer science, bioinformatics, or applied mathematics;
  • Technical proficiency in developing scalable algorithms for large-scale biological datasets;
  • Practical experience with NGS technologies (RNA-Seq, WGS);
  • A drive to address critical questions in medical immunogenomics and reduce health disparities;
  • Strong commitment to reproducible science and public-facing genomic tools.
