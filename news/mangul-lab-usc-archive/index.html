Explore Our Previous Research: Mangul Lab at USC

Before joining Jagiellonian University, the Mangul Lab was based at the University of Southern California (USC). Explore our past news and milestones from our time in Los Angeles.

The foundation of our current research was built during our productive years at the University of Southern California (USC). As we embark on our new journey at the Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology in Kraków, we remain proud of the milestones, publications, and community engagement achieved by our team in Los Angeles.

From developing novel computational methods to leading international bioinformatics workshops, the Mangul Lab at USC was a hub of innovation and education. We invite our colleagues, students, and collaborators to explore the full archive of our activities, news, and updates from the USC era.

What you can find in the archive:

  • Announcements of major research grants and awards (NSF CAREER, Fulbright);
  • Highlights of student achievements and graduations;
  • Recaps of lab retreats and international conference presentations;
  • Media coverage and outreach initiatives.
