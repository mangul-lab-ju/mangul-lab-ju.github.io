Open Position: Postdoc (F/M) in Mangul Group

The Mangul Lab at the Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology is looking for a talented Postdoctoral researcher / Staff Scientist to join our team in Kraków.

The Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology (MCB) of the Jagiellonian University is seeking a dedicated Postdoc / Staff Scientist to join the newly formed research group led by Dr. Serghei Mangul.

We are looking for excellent scientists who love to work in research and solve complex problems. This position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to high-impact computational genomics research in an international environment.

General Requirements

The selection procedure is open to individuals who meet the following eligibility criteria:

  • Holding at least a doctoral degree (PhD);
  • Having relevant scientific achievements;
  • Taking an active part in scientific life.

Essential Skills & Experience

We are looking for candidates with a strong quantitative background and a passion for reproducible science:

  • Education: Preferred PhD in Computer Science or a related quantitative field. An additional degree in Biology is a significant advantage.
  • Research Record: A minimum of 6 months of international research experience and a proven track record of peer-reviewed publications in high-impact journals (e.g., Nature portfolio).
  • Technical Skills: Advanced programming in Python, R, and Unix/Linux environments. Expert-level ability to develop reproducible analytical pipelines.
  • Data Expertise: Deep familiarity with modern sequencing technologies (short-read and long-read) and metagenomic workflows.
  • Communication: Fluency in English with the ability to present complex multidisciplinary topics clearly.

About the Position

  • Unit: Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology (MCB)
  • Position: Assistant Professor (F/M) / Postdoc
  • Application Deadline: May 23, 2026

This position involves employment for an initial 3-month trial period, followed by a 9-month fixed-term contract, with the possibility of extension based on performance.

SOURCE INFORMATION & APPLICATION This position is open at the Małopolska Centre of Biotechnology. For full details, please refer to the official job advertisement and the application procedure on the Jagiellonian University website.
